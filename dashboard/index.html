How the board is built

One snapshot in, one ranking out. No names are special-cased: the same function scores every row, and the comparables come from this snapshot alone.

  1. Comparables. For each category with at least 3 live-token protocols, comp is the median market-cap ÷ TVL of those protocols; otherwise the whole market's median is used. Multiples are clamped to 0.02–1.00.
  2. Base. base = TVL × comp — what the market pays per dollar of TVL, for this kind of protocol.
  3. Growth. growth = 1 + clamp(7d %, −60, 120) ÷ 100 × 0.5 — a protocol growing fast is worth more than one shrinking.
  4. Maturity. maturity = 0.70 + min(age, 1095 days) ÷ 1095 × 0.30 — longer with no token, more pent-up value. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  5. Breadth. breadth = 1 + min(chains − 1, 9) × 0.01 — a protocol live on more chains reaches more users.
  6. Implied value. base × growth × maturity × breadth, rounded to whole dollars.

Who gets ranked

The board covers the snapshot's whole 40-protocol watchlist, then every other protocol that records no token and passes the watchlist's own exclusion rule without its $50M floor: no exchange, no bridge, no liquid staking or restaking, no versioned pool, no wrapped or staked product, no exchange-branded product. Protocols that record a token are set aside in the Have a token view.

What “records no token” means

DefiLlama's record is not proof: a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. The board ranks what the record shows and says so on every row — it never claims a protocol has no token, only that none is recorded.
